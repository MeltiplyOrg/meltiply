Meltiply does not host an interactive AI Studio (`/agent`) or an Integrations directory (`/plugs`). Both routes and their sidebar navigation items have been removed from the application, along with all simulated demonstration flows and mock configuration dialogs.

Visiting `/agent` or `/plugs` redirects to the posts hub on the [Publishing](/docs/publishing) screen.

## Campaign drafting and scheduling

Real post drafting and multi-channel scheduling are handled directly in the **Publishing Composer** on the [Publishing](/docs/publishing) screen:

- **Compose shared copy** — draft the primary text published across all selected destinations.
- **Platform-specific options** — set channel-specific fields (such as YouTube titles and TikTok privacy settings) in the composer drawer.
- **Attach media** — upload images or video directly in the composer.
- **Schedule or draft** — queue posts for scheduled delivery or save them as drafts scoped to the active Brand Profile.
- **Track scheduled posts** — review and manage upcoming scheduled deliveries on the Posts screen.

## Connected social platforms

Meltiply does not host a directory of third-party automation or agent plugins.

Social platform accounts are connected and managed exclusively on the [Channels](/docs/channels) board. Supported platforms (such as YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and Threads) connect via standard OAuth authorization flows and are organized by Brand Profile.
