**Analytics** is a standalone workspace page at `/analytics`, listed between
**Posts** and **Channels**. It is read-only and follows the active Brand
Profile; choose **All Brand Profiles** through the workspace scope to compare
the whole workspace.

## Read the cohort, not activity in the range

The page measures Posts published inside the selected range. Each published
destination contributes its latest stored snapshot at or before the range end.

- A Post published before the range is excluded, even if it still receives
  engagement during the range.
- A Post published inside the range shows cumulative metrics as of the range
  end, not the change since the range started.
- If one Post published to several Channels, their latest snapshots are summed.
- **Published destinations** counts each published Distribution once, so one
  Post may contribute several destinations.
- Analytics sync stops after 366 days. An older Distribution keeps contributing
  its latest retained snapshot.
- Range boundaries and chart buckets use UTC; there is no workspace timezone
  setting.

The page states this under the totals. The range presets are the last 7, 30,
90, and 366 days; the default is 30 days. Range, platform, and metric are kept
in the URL so a view can be bookmarked and restored with browser back/forward.

## Metrics

| Metric                 | Meaning                                                                          |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Views                  | Views reported by the selected platforms.                                        |
| Likes                  | Likes reported for the cohort.                                                   |
| Comments               | Comments reported for the cohort.                                                |
| Shares                 | Shares, reposts, and quotes reported for the cohort.                             |
| Published destinations | Published Distributions in the cohort; each Post-to-Channel outcome counts once. |

The **Platform breakdown** also shows Reach, Impressions, Engagement rate,
Followers, and Follower gain. Engagement rate is likes, comments, and shares
divided by views; it is unavailable when there are no measured views.
Followers are account-level measurements, not a sum of Post audiences; follower
gain needs a complete baseline. Saves and Clicks columns appear only when a
Provider reports them; otherwise a footnote identifies them as **Not tracked**.
Watch time is not displayed.

All five supported Providers report stored analytics when their
analytics scope is granted: Instagram and Threads media insights, Facebook Page
Reel insights, TikTok video counters, and YouTube Analytics. Threads engagement
reporting can still be disabled by the deployment's acceptance configuration.
The page never fabricates data for a Provider that has rejected or not granted
analytics access.

Provider limits still apply. Facebook Reels use Video Insights, which requires
the `read_insights` and `pages_manage_engagement` permissions; shares are
derived from Meta's combined comments-and-shares metric. YouTube Analytics
reports lifetime aggregates for the uploaded video and shares Google's quota.
TikTok reports the creator's current video counters. After an analytics scope is
added, reconnect the Channel so the Provider issues a token that can read it.
TikTok and YouTube analytics permissions are optional: declining them does not
prevent a publishing-capable Channel from connecting, refreshing, or publishing.
Reconnect to add those permissions when analytics are needed.

## Inspect one Post

In **Posts → Grid**, each card has a compact bottom row for views, likes,
comments, and shares, with an icon before each value. Counts stay on one line;
drafts, unpublished Posts, and unmeasured counters show a dash rather than zero.

Posts loads 60 records at a time. In **Grid**, use **Previous page** and **Next
page** beneath the cards; each page shows at most 60 cards. In **Table**, **Load
more** appends the next batch without replacing existing rows. Filters apply to
the loaded Posts and reset the Grid to page one. Fetched pages are reused only
in the signed-in browser's memory across view and route changes; each page is
fresh for one minute and inactive pages expire after 30 minutes. Nothing is
saved across reloads. Publishing progress and your mutations update cached data.
Progress-only reads do not extend a page's collection freshness. A removed active
Post is pruned without blocking other outcomes; failed detail reads retain their
last known data and report an error while later polls retry.

Channels and active Brand Profiles remain fresh for five minutes. Analytics
dashboard and rankings share the same five-minute cohort, and **Refresh** advances
both together. Private cache data is scoped to your current user and Workspace,
cleared when identity changes, and never persisted to browser storage or a shared
CDN. Other members' changes can wait for refresh or freshness expiry.

Open a published Post from **Posts** to see its latest totals and two line charts:

- **Engagement over time** shows likes, comments, and shares.
- **Reach & impressions over time** shows three separate lines: reach, impressions,
  and media views.

**Reach** is the unique audience reported within each Provider's scope.
**Impressions** count displays of the content, including repeat displays to the
same person. **Views** count media views or plays; the precise qualifying rules
depend on the Provider. These are separate counters, not interchangeable metrics.

The charts use stored measurement times and cumulative counters, not live activity
or activity gained during each interval. Between Channel syncs, each Channel keeps
its last measured value; historical snapshots are never added together. Reach is
not deduplicated across Channels. A single measurement appears as a point rather
than a fabricated trend. Untracked metrics are labelled instead of plotted as zero.
The inspector shows up to the first 500 measurements per Channel and displays a
notice if that bound is reached. Failed history reads have a Retry action.

## Coverage and freshness

Missing measurement is never displayed as zero:

- **Not supported** means every destination in that row belongs to a platform
  whose Provider does not report analytics.
- **Partial** means a row mixes supported and unsupported destinations; the
  shown sum covers only the supported destinations.
- **Awaiting sync** means a published destination has no stored snapshot yet.
- **Awaiting first sync** lists supported platforms whose snapshots have not
  arrived.

A partial-data banner names any unsupported platforms in scope. The page shows
the exact last successful sync time and warns when it is older than 26 hours,
the daily sync cadence plus margin. **Refresh** re-reads stored metrics; it does
not trigger a Provider sync.

These freshness intervals apply to browser reads only. They do not change server
authorization or trigger Provider synchronization.

## Chart and table

**Engagement by platform** stacks likes, comments, and shares in one horizontal
bar per measured platform. Its tooltip and screen-reader table include the
total engagement, views, and published-destination count.

**Performance trend** shows cumulative measured changes in views, likes,
comments, and shares, indexed to 100 at each metric's first measured period.
Hover for absolute cumulative values; the index is not a percentage growth rate.
**Posts over time** stacks publication counts by platform. Both charts offer
Day, Week, and Month grouping.

**Follower evolution** shows stored account follower histories by platform and
net gain when the baseline is complete. **Content format breakdown** compares
posts, per-post averages, and totals for formats derived from published
Distributions' frozen attachments.

**Best time to post** is a weekday-by-hour UTC heatmap of average engagement,
likes, or views by publication time. A recommendation requires at least two
measured Posts in a slot; it is historical evidence, not a forecast.

**Platform breakdown** includes Posts, Views, Likes, Comments, Shares, Reach,
Impressions, Engagement rate, Followers, Follower gain, and conditional
Saves/Clicks columns. Supported platforms with no content snapshot show
**Awaiting sync** rather than measured zeros; missing follower values remain
unavailable. Its wide table scrolls within its own card on phones.

**Top posts** ranks the cohort by the selected metric, then publication time,
then Post identity. Each row shows the Post title or excerpt, platforms and
destination count, publication time, and all four metrics. Metric column
headers re-rank the table. **Load more** raises the top-N window in steps of ten
up to fifty. On phones the table becomes stacked rows instead of scrolling the
whole page sideways.

## API consumers

The same read model is available to API clients:

- `GET /api/v1/analytics` — platform-level totals for a published cohort.
- `GET /api/v1/analytics/dashboard` — cohort summary, time series, platform and
  format breakdowns, follower history, and best-time cells.
- `GET /api/v1/analytics/posts` — ranked Posts with stable `limit`/`offset`
  pagination.
- `GET /api/v1/analytics/{distribution_id}` — retained snapshots for one
  Distribution.

All four are scoped to the authenticated Workspace. The aggregate endpoints
respect the optional `brand_profile_id` filter; Distribution history checks
ownership of the requested Distribution.
