Meltiply plans set limits on media uploads, scheduled distributions, concurrent platform connections, and media storage.

The web application does not host a dedicated `/billing` page, in-app usage meters, self-serve checkout buttons, or billing portal links. Navigating to `/billing` redirects to the posts workspace. Your current plan tier appears under the workspace name at the top of the sidebar.

## Plans at a glance

Meltiply supports three plan tiers:

- **Free** — included with every workspace, no payment required.
- **Creator** — entry paid tier for individual creators with higher distribution and upload allowances.
- **Agency** — tier for higher publishing volume and multi-channel workflows.

The backend tracks subscription statuses: **Free**, **Trialing**, **Active**, **Past due**, and **Cancelled**. Paid-plan allowances apply while the subscription status is Active or Trialing. If a subscription lapses or is cancelled, the workspace operates under Free-plan limits.

## Usage limits

Each plan defines limits for:

- **Uploads** — media assets added to your workspace per usage period.
- **Distributions** — posts published or scheduled to connected platforms per usage period.
- **Platforms** — social accounts you can connect concurrently.
- **Storage** — total space your uploaded media can occupy.
- **Analytics** — whether post performance sync is enabled on the backend.
- **Inbox replies** — whether replying to comments from the Inbox is enabled on the backend.

## Reaching limits

The backend enforces plan limits when you upload media, schedule distributions, or connect platform accounts. An operation that would exceed its limit is rejected; changing the browser's local state cannot bypass that check.

The current app does not provide approaching-quota banners or a live usage dashboard. Do not rely on the sidebar plan label to show remaining capacity.

## Backend billing and subscriptions

Backend billing endpoints (`/api/v1/billing/plan` and `/api/v1/billing/usage`) track reconciled usage and plan limits, and Polar billing integration powers checkout and customer portal session creation in the API worker.

Self-serve subscription checkout and billing portal management are not available directly within the frontend interface.
