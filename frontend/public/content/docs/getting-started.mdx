Each Meltiply installation has one owner. First-run setup appears only until that owner is created; it does not change how Workspaces and Brand Profiles organize publishing.

## Create an account

1. Open the app at `/sign-in` on an empty installation.
2. Enter the private **Setup secret** supplied by your deployment administrator, **Your name**, **Email address**, and a unique **Password** of 8–128 characters.
3. Select **Create owner account**.
4. Follow the verification link sent to your email, then return to sign in.

After setup, registration closes and the page becomes the owner's sign-in form. The setup secret is not your login password. If the verification email is missing, use **Resend verification** on the sign-in form.

## Sign in

1. Go to `/sign-in`.
2. Enter your **Email address** and **Password**.
3. Select **Sign in**. If an authenticator is enabled, enter its current code or a saved, unused backup code.

Successful sign-in lands on **Posts**. Use **Forgot password?** to request an expiring reset link; password reset does not bypass an enabled authenticator.

Open **Settings → Security** from desktop or mobile navigation to change your password or enable an authenticator. Enrollment requires a live code before activation. Save backup codes offline: each works once, and replacing them invalidates the previous set. Password reauthentication is required for sensitive security changes.

To sign out, select the sign-out control beside your account identity. Signing out revokes your session, but does not cancel already scheduled publishing.

## Your first five minutes

Work through these steps in order — each builds on the previous one:

1. **Create a Brand Profile.** Every connection and Post belongs to a Brand Profile. In **Channels**, admins can choose **Create Brand Profile**; existing profiles appear in the page's Brand Profiles tab. See [Workspaces & Brand Profiles](/docs/workspaces).
2. **Connect a channel.** Go to **Channels → Add account**, choose a Brand Profile, and connect a social account. See [Channels](/docs/channels).
3. **Compose a Post.** Open **Posts → Create post**, select the owning Brand Profile and destination channels, and add text and media. See [Publishing](/docs/publishing) and [Media](/docs/media).
4. **Publish or schedule.** Save a draft, schedule a date and time, or publish immediately from **Posts**. See [Publishing](/docs/publishing).

> Creating Brand Profiles requires a **workspace admin** role. If you're a member, ask your admin to create the profile — the Channels page indicates when this is needed.

## Find your way around

### Sidebar

The warm, compact sidebar groups your publishing work:

- **Posts** (`/posts`) — one sidebar destination for composing, scheduling, and inspecting publication outcomes. Choose **Table**, **Grid**, or **Calendar** inside the Posts toolbar; Table is the default.
- **Channels** (`/channels`) — manage connected accounts in an **Accounts** directory or review actual **Brand Profiles** as cards with their connection counts.
- **Brand Profiles** — select a real profile to scope your work, or **All Brand Profiles** for aggregate review.
- **Settings** (`/settings`) — appearance, language, account details, and security.

The desktop rail collapses to an icon rail. The workspace name at its top is read-only; it is not a workspace switcher.

### Search and jump

Select **Search** in the sidebar or **Search or jump to…** in the top bar. The keyboard shortcut is Command-K on Apple devices and Control-K elsewhere. Type to find a page or loaded Brand Profile, use Tab to reach a result, and press Enter to select it. Escape closes the palette.

This is navigation search, not a global Post index. Search Post captions and filter Channels with the toolbar on their own pages.

### Settings and account

Open **Settings** from the desktop sidebar or mobile navigation. Choose an Appearance or Language section, review read-only Account details, or open the existing Security controls. Theme/language persistence requires functional cookie consent. Sign out from the sidebar account area or Settings → Account.

### On smaller screens

The persistent 52px top bar replaces the desktop rail with a navigation menu. Search remains in the top bar; the menu opens Posts, Channels, and Settings. Collection controls wrap without hiding search or view selection. Settings sections become a horizontal row; account security and sign out remain reachable.
