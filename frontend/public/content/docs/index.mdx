Meltiply is a single workspace for planning, scheduling, and publishing social media content. Connect your social accounts, organize them under Brand Profiles, and build posts in the composer — without hopping between tools.

## Start here

New to Meltiply? Follow these guides in order:

1. **[Getting started](/docs/getting-started)** — create your account, learn the layout, and complete first-time setup.
2. **[Workspaces & Brand Profiles](/docs/workspaces)** — understand how work is organized and scoped.
3. **[Channels](/docs/channels)** — connect your social accounts and keep them healthy.
4. **[Media](/docs/media)** — composer-owned uploads, progress, and attachments.
5. **[Publishing](/docs/publishing)** — compose, schedule, and ship posts.

## Find your way around

The sidebar provides direct access to the primary workspace surfaces:

| Area     | What it's for                                                                               |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Channels | Manage account connections in the Accounts directory or review Brand Profiles (`/channels`) |
| Posts    | Compose, schedule, and track real posts in Table, Grid, or Calendar (`/posts`)              |
| Settings | Change supported appearance, language, account, and security preferences (`/settings`)      |

The warm sidebar also lists Brand Profiles. Posts is a single entry; its Table, Grid, and Calendar selector lives on the page. Search or jump from the sidebar, top bar, or Command/Control-K. Settings is a full workspace page; sign out from the sidebar account area or Settings → Account. Collapse the rail with the control beside the workspace name.
