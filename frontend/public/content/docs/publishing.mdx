The **Posts** screen is the publishing hub. It lists everything you have drafted, scheduled, or published, and hosts the **Publishing Composer**, where posts are written and sent to your channels.

## Views

The page toolbar orders **Table → Calendar → Grid**. **Table** is the default; Posts remains a single sidebar destination. Creating or editing a Post opens the composer workflow, not another collection view.

- **Table** — a spreadsheet-density collection with a sticky metadata header and collapsible counted groups. Group by publication **Status**, **Brand Profile**, or **None**. Use **Columns** to show or hide Status, Channels, Publish date, Brand Profile, and the analytics columns **Likes**, **Cmts**, **Shrs**, **Saves**, **Clicks**, **Views**, **Impr.**, and **Reach**. Published Posts show stored measured values; drafts, unpublished Posts, missing measurements, and untracked metrics show `-`. See [Analytics](/docs/analytics) for aggregation and availability.
- **Calendar** — Gr8r-style **Month** and **Week** layouts for actual publishing dates. Use **Today** or previous/next controls to navigate. Month shows up to three compact Post chips per day; **+N more** or the day number opens that day's selectable Post list. Week shows all Posts in seven date columns. Select a Post chip to inspect it; a day's **+** opens the composer with that date pre-filled when a Brand Profile is selected. Calendar does not drag-reschedule existing Posts.
- **Grid** — a media-card gallery of the same Posts with an adjustable column count.

Open **Filter** to add Platform, Brand Profile, Post status, or Publish date rows, then choose their values. Empty added rows do not change results; supported filters combine with **and**. Remove a row with its trash control, reset the builder with **Clear all**, or remove an applied value through its chip. Collection search stays separate from top-bar jump search. Sort by newest/oldest; Table grouping and column visibility change presentation without changing Post data.

Platform, Brand Profile, and Post status use checkbox lists that stay open while you select several values. A Post matches **any** selected value within one field, and must also match the other active fields. **All Brand Profiles**, **All statuses**, or clearing the last check restores that field's full collection. Selecting exactly one Brand Profile also scopes new composition; several profiles remain a review filter, not multiple owners for one Post.

On phones, swipe the Table or Calendar date canvas sideways to reach additional columns or days; the page itself stays within the screen. The Table's empty message and Create post action stay visible without horizontal scrolling. Calendar's Today, date navigation, and Month/Week controls stay outside the scrolling dates.

Checkboxes select Posts in Table and Grid; Calendar day details expose the same
controls. A select-all checkbox is checked only when every visible filtered Post
is selected; partial selections leave it visually unchecked. Selection survives
view/filter changes and collapsing a group. The inverse-ink floating pill
shows the actual count, **Delete**, and an icon-only **Deselect** button. Confirm the
exact batch before deletion. Successful deletions leave the selection; failed
Posts remain selected for retry.

Deletion uses each Post's owning Brand Profile, so individual and bulk deletion
also work in **All Brand Profiles**. Changing the active Brand Profile clears
selection. A successful deletion removes the Post from Table, Calendar, and Grid,
including after switching views or reloading. It never removes published platform
copies; retained publication history does not bring the Post back into Calendar.

## Before you publish

A Post always has one owning Brand Profile. In **Destinations**, select one or several profiles using the checkbox list. Composing once for several profiles saves **one independent Post per selected profile**, not a Post with several owners. Each copy has its own media, channels, delivery outcomes, and subsequent edits. The composer preserves a concrete profile choice or initially selects the first available profile; reopening a Post selects its actual owner.

Drafts can be saved without destination channels. Scheduling and Publish Now require at least one eligible channel **for every selected profile**. Channels are grouped by provider across the selected profiles. Each card identifies its Brand Profile, account photo or initials, and provider. Separate **Username: @handle** and **ID: provider-assigned identifier** rows remain visible, including for unavailable connections. The ID is assigned by the platform; it is not the username or Meltiply's internal record ID. Missing usernames say **Username not provided** rather than guessing. Long values wrap on phones. Recover disabled, expired, or incomplete connections on [Channels](/docs/channels), or use **Add Channel**.

## Composing a post

Click **Create post** (or **Edit** on an existing draft or scheduled post) to open the composer.

- **Shared Content** is the text published to every selected channel. The counter under the editor shows used/maximum characters using the tightest selected platform limit, plus how many channels you're targeting. Supported providers retain their declared caption limits even before live publishing is enabled.
- **Add Media** attaches images or videos directly from your device (via file picker or drag and drop). Attached media can be reordered, replaced, paused, resumed, or removed directly in the composer. See [Media](/docs/media).
- With multiple Brand Profiles, each profile receives its own uploaded copy of every attachment. These copies count against the workspace's media limits; channels never reference another profile's media.
- Media attachments are validated against each channel's constraints (accepted media types and allowed attachment counts).
- **Per-channel sections** follow shared text and media in the same scrollable editor, with provider logos in their headings and no account-name tags or tabs. Each channel's caption is editable immediately and follows **Shared Content** until you change it, including clearing it intentionally. **Remove Custom Override** appears only for edited captions and restores the shared text.
- **Channel settings** — each channel's section provides persistent provider configuration:

| Platform | Controls configured with the post                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| YouTube  | **Video Title** (required, 2–100 characters) and **Tags** (comma-separated; combined length limited to 500 characters, count capped by channel capabilities).                                                                                                                                                    |
| TikTok   | **Post Title** (optional), **Privacy Level** (required — options determined by creator account requirements), interaction toggles (**Duet**, **Stitch**, and **Comments** — enabled when supported by the creator account), and disclosure toggles (**Brand Content**, **Organic Brand**, and **AI Generated**). |
| Threads  | Informational panel — Threads posts use standard post text and attached media with no provider-specific controls.                                                                                                                                                                                                |

Facebook and Instagram channels publish using standard post text and attached media without provider-specific configuration panels in the composer.

### Validation

When you submit, the composer checks that profiles are selected and the composition has text or media. Scheduling or publishing additionally requires at least one selected channel **per profile** and a valid date/time for **Schedule**; **Save Drafts** needs neither channels nor a schedule. Channel-specific text, media, and provider settings must satisfy their platform limits. Problems appear in a banner without discarding your work.

With functional cookie consent, browser-local recovery retains text, attachments, overrides, selected profiles/channels, and the saved copy identities for the active workspace/profile. Without that optional storage, composition and server-backed draft saving still work; do not rely on browser recovery after leaving. Successful saving clears the local recovery copy. See [Cookies and storage](/docs/cookies).

Inspecting another Post does not discard your open composition: its text, settings, and active uploads stay in memory while Details is shown. Close Details or choose **New Post** in its header to resume that composition. This does not require cookie consent; closing the composer or leaving the Posts route still releases its live state.

## Draft, Queue, Schedule, or Publish Now

- **Save Draft** — stores the post text without sending it anywhere. No channel selection is needed. Drafts appear in the **Drafts** status filter in Posts and can be edited, scheduled, or deleted later. Saving a draft for a post that was scheduled cancels its pending deliveries.
- **Queue** — assigns the next free future slot from a named weekly plan in the Post's single owning Brand Profile. Select a Queue, not a date and time. Text, media, selected Channels, and their settings remain part of the same Post.
- **Schedule** — queues the post for the date and time in the picker.
- **Publish Now** — queues the post for immediate delivery to every selected channel.

"Queued" is a Channel delivery state, not membership in a named **Queue**. It means Meltiply accepted delivery for that Channel; Platform outcomes are reported individually — one Channel can publish while another is still processing or has failed.

For several profiles, all drafts are saved before any are queued. Per-profile progress identifies successes and failures. A failed save leaves completed drafts intact and queues nothing new. If queuing partially succeeds, retry the unchanged composition to finish the remaining profiles without sending completed copies again. Repeated draft saves update the same copies rather than creating duplicates. After closing the composition, manage each saved Post independently.

Choose **Schedule**, then select the **Date** button to open the compact calendar. Use the month arrows to browse; keyboard users can move with arrow keys, Home/End, or Page Up/Down and select with Enter. Selecting a day closes the calendar; Escape dismisses it without changing the schedule or closing the composer. For **Time**, click the clock button to choose an hour and exact minute, then **Done**. Choices apply immediately. To type instead, click the field's left padding or hour digits and enter a 24-hour time such as `14:30`; click minute digits to edit only minutes. Up/Down adjusts the focused segment and Left/Right moves between segments. Your browser's local time zone stays visible underneath. Changing the date preserves the time, changing time preserves the date, and browsing the calendar does not change the selected schedule. Calendar entry points retain their pre-filled date and 10:00 local time.

Wide composers give destinations and publishing a compact column, leaving more room for writing. On phones, the same sections stack; date and time remain together, and the calendar opens only when needed. Destination cards use compact rounded-square account portraits, with the name and platform together above the username and ID. The composer keeps its save explanation and primary action in a pinned bar; scroll the body to reach destinations, provider settings, and scheduling without losing that action.

## Weekly Queues

Open **Queues**, choose the owning Brand Profile, and select **Create Queue**. Give the plan a name, a supported IANA timezone such as `America/New_York`, and one or more weekday/time slots. Times use 24-hour hours and minutes; duplicate slots are refused. Admins manage configuration, Editors and Admins can enqueue or remove Posts, and Viewers can inspect plans and outcomes.

In the composer, keep exactly one Brand Profile selected, choose **Queue**, select its plan, and use **Add to Queue**. Queue mode does not make independent copies across several profiles; use Draft, Schedule, or Now for those workflows. Reopen the Queue and choose **Inspect Posts** to see assigned times and per-Channel outcomes after reload. Post Details also retains membership and its assigned instant.

The Queue calendar opens in **Week**. Switch between **Month**, **Week**, and
**Day**, use the arrows to browse, or choose **Today** to return to the current
date in the Queue's timezone. Selecting a month date opens its Day timeline.
Calendar chips show actual assigned Posts, not speculative future slot events.
Choose a Post to inspect its assigned time and independent Channel outcomes,
open the full Post, or request safe removal.

Week and Day have an hourly timeline with a **red current-time line** when the
displayed dates include today. It advances every 30 seconds and refreshes when
you return to the tab. The calendar initially scrolls near now, but clock updates
do not move your scroll position. Month highlights today instead of drawing an
hourly line across date cells. **Next in this view** identifies the next pending
assignment in the displayed window; it does not imply that other dates are empty.
All dates, the clock, and the time line use the selected Queue's named timezone,
not your device's timezone. Repeated DST-hour Posts retain distinct offset labels;
the timeline represents wall-clock time, not event durations or rescheduling.

### Allocation and timezones

- The earliest free occurrence strictly after allocation time wins. A slot exactly at the current instant, a past occurrence, or an occupied reservation is skipped. When the week's remaining slots are full or past, allocation rolls into the following week.
- Sequential submissions reserve successive available slots in accepted transaction order. Concurrent submissions are serialized by the existing Workspace scheduling lock; the database also enforces one active membership per Post and one reservation per Queue/instant. Concurrent arrival order is not a promise about browser click order.
- A replay of the same request returns its saved result without reserving or publishing again. Enqueuing the same Post into the same Queue with another key keeps its still-future reservation and existing Channel delivery identities. Remove it before choosing another Queue or reusing an elapsed reservation.
- Weekly slots are interpreted in the Queue's timezone, not the browser's timezone. Assigned instants are stored in UTC. DST spring-forward gaps skip that week's nonexistent local time; fall-back overlaps use the later, post-transition occurrence, once. For example, New York `02:30` on March 8, 2026 is skipped, and `01:30` on November 1, 2026 reserves `06:30Z`, not `05:30Z`.

### Changes, removal, and deletion

Editing a name, timezone, or slot plan affects only new allocations. Existing memberships retain their original name snapshot and UTC instant; editing a plan never silently moves or republishes a Post.

**Remove from Queue** cancels only safely unclaimed pending Channel deliveries, releases that slot, and retains the Post's text, Assets, targets, and settings. Other reservations do not shift forward. Once a Provider attempt, active lease, published copy, cancellation request, or uncertain outcome owns any Channel, removal is refused; inspect and resolve those outcomes instead. Removing a Post that was already locally cancelled is safe only if no Provider ever owned its work.

Cancelling an individual Channel or saving a Queue-owned Post as a draft does not release its reservation. Use explicit removal before switching to manual Schedule or Publish Now, or before independently rescheduling a Channel. Deleting the stored Post releases membership while retaining normal delivery history. No action unpublishes an existing Platform copy.

**Delete Queue** retires a plan only after its Posts have resolved or been safely removed. Pending, in-flight, and uncertain work prevents deletion. Retired plans disappear from selection; historical membership and Platform outcomes remain unchanged on their Posts. Remove a safely cancelled Post before retirement if you want to reuse that composition with a different schedule.

Queue-assigned work uses the same durable Schedule, Due work, Outbox, and Provider delivery path as manual publishing. Failure, eligible retry, cancellation, Ambiguous outcomes, and Reconciliation retain their existing meanings. The assigned time is a target, not a guarantee of Platform delivery.

## Post statuses

Each post shows a status per channel in the post details (open any post to see them). The group badge summarizes the most urgent channel state.

| Status                                  | Meaning                                                                                                   |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Scheduled                               | Waiting for its scheduled time.                                                                           |
| Queued / Processing / Awaiting provider | Delivery is in progress for that channel. Statuses update automatically while posts are in flight.        |
| Published                               | Delivered to the channel. A **View on platform** link opens the live post when the platform provides one. |
| Failed                                  | Delivery failed for that channel — the reason is shown.                                                   |
| Cancellation requested                  | Meltiply is stopping an in-flight delivery — check whether it still published on the platform.            |
| Cancelled                               | The delivery was stopped before it went out.                                                              |
| Needs reconciliation                    | Meltiply could not confirm the outcome with the platform.                                                 |

## Managing posts

Click a Table row, Grid card, or Calendar Post chip—or choose **Details** in a Calendar day's list—to open the side drawer. Its compact header identifies the Post and exposes supported actions. Flat properties, the full caption, ordered attachments, and real Channel outcomes share one scrollable body; this is not an editable task/status panel.

The inspector is a non-modal edge panel, filling the viewport on phones. Close with its header's **Close** icon or **Escape**; the workspace stays clickable, and outside clicks do not dismiss it. Closing inspection returns to an interrupted composition when one exists; otherwise focus returns to its opener without moving the collection's scroll position, even if you scrolled the list while inspecting.

Table has no trailing actions column. Open Details and choose **Edit post** for an editable Post; its header exposes deletion and, when applicable, **Cancel all pending**, with confirmation. Grid cards and Calendar day entries retain direct Edit/Delete shortcuts. Provider publication outcomes cannot be manually marked complete.

Available actions depend on the channel's state:

- **Retry** — re-queues delivery for a **Failed** channel. Not every failure is retryable; when it isn't, the retry is refused with an error toast and the failure stays on the post.
- **Reschedule** — picks a new date and time for eligible delivery (also offered for unconfirmed deliveries). A Queue-owned Post must first be safely removed from its Queue.
- **Cancel** / **Cancel all pending** — stops pending deliveries. For a channel already in flight, this sends a cancellation request rather than a guaranteed stop — watch the status and check the platform. A confirmation dialog explains that only pending channels are cancelled; channels that already published are unaffected and the post stays live there. Cancelling never unpublishes a post that already went out.
- **Edit post** — opens a draft or a Post with eligible pending channels in the composer. Removing a channel removes its pending delivery. Published Channel outcomes remain observed results and cannot be manually rewritten.
- **Delete post / Delete draft** — removes the stored Post and stops its pending channels after confirmation. Anything already published remains on its platform; remove that copy on the platform itself.

> **Heads-up on unconfirmed deliveries:** if a channel shows **Awaiting provider** or **Needs reconciliation**, check the post directly on that platform before sending it again — the delivery may have actually gone through, and reposting could create a duplicate.
