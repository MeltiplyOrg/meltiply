The **Posts** screen is the publishing hub. It lists everything you have drafted, scheduled, or published, and hosts the **Publishing Composer**, where posts are written and sent to your channels.

## Views

The page toolbar orders **Table → Calendar → Grid**. **Table** is the default; Posts remains a single sidebar destination. Creating or editing a Post opens the composer workflow, not another collection view.

- **Table** — a spreadsheet-density collection with a sticky metadata header and collapsible counted groups. Group by publication **Status**, **Brand Profile**, or **None**. Use **Columns** to show or hide Status, Channels, Publish date, Brand Profile, and the analytics columns **Likes**, **Cmts**, **Shrs**, **Saves**, **Clicks**, **Views**, **Impr.**, and **Reach**. Published Posts show stored measured values; drafts, unpublished Posts, missing measurements, and untracked metrics show `-`. See [Analytics](/docs/analytics) for aggregation and availability.
- **Calendar** — Gr8r-style **Month** and **Week** layouts for actual publishing dates. Use **Today** or previous/next controls to navigate. Month shows up to three compact Post chips per day; **+N more** or the day number opens that day's selectable Post list. Week shows all Posts in seven date columns. Select a Post chip to inspect it; a day's **+** opens the composer with that date pre-filled when a Brand Profile is selected. Calendar does not drag-reschedule existing Posts.
- **Grid** — a media-card gallery of the same Posts with an adjustable column count.

Open **Filter** to add Platform, Brand Profile, Post status, or Publish date rows, then choose their values. Empty added rows do not change results; supported filters combine with **and**. Remove a row with its trash control, reset the builder with **Clear all**, or remove an applied value through its chip. Collection search stays separate from top-bar jump search. Sort by newest/oldest; Table grouping and column visibility change presentation without changing Post data.

Checkboxes select Posts in Table and Grid; Calendar day details expose the same
controls. A select-all checkbox is checked only when every visible filtered Post
is selected; partial selections leave it visually unchecked. Selection survives
view/filter changes and collapsing a group. The inverse-ink floating pill
shows the actual count, **Delete**, and an icon-only **Deselect** button. Confirm the
exact batch before deletion. Successful deletions leave the selection; failed
Posts remain selected for retry.

Deletion uses each Post's owning Brand Profile, so individual and bulk deletion
also work in **All Brand Profiles**. Changing the active Brand Profile clears
selection. A successful deletion removes the Post from Table, Calendar, and Grid,
including after switching views or reloading. It never removes published platform
copies; retained publication history does not bring the Post back into Calendar.

## Before you publish

A Post needs one owning Brand Profile and text or media. The composer preserves your selected profile or chooses the first available profile for a new Post. Reopening a Post from **All Brand Profiles** selects its actual owner. Change the profile in **Destinations** when needed; aggregate scope is for review, not publishing under multiple owners.

Drafts can be saved without destination channels. Scheduling and Publish Now additionally require at least one eligible channel from the composer's **Destinations** list. Every channel belonging to the selected profile appears with its account photo, or account initials when the photo is missing or cannot load; a small provider badge identifies its platform separately. Below the display name and platform, each card shows separate **Username: @handle** and **ID: provider-assigned identifier** rows, including for unavailable connections. The ID is assigned by the platform; it is not the username or Meltiply's internal record ID. If the platform supplied no username, the card says **Username not provided** rather than guessing one from the display name or ID. Long values wrap so they stay readable on phones. Disabled, expired, or incomplete connections must be recovered on [Channels](/docs/channels), or connected through **Add Channel**.

## Composing a post

Click **Create post** (or **Edit** on an existing draft or scheduled post) to open the composer.

- **Shared Content** is the text published to every selected channel. The counter under the editor shows used/maximum characters using the tightest selected platform limit, plus how many channels you're targeting. Supported providers retain their declared caption limits even before live publishing is enabled.
- **Add Media** attaches images or videos directly from your device (via file picker or drag and drop). Attached media can be reordered, replaced, paused, resumed, or removed directly in the composer. See [Media](/docs/media).
- Media attachments are validated against each channel's constraints (accepted media types and allowed attachment counts).
- **Per-channel sections** follow shared text and media in the same scrollable editor, with provider logos in their headings and no account-name tags or tabs. Each channel's caption is editable immediately and follows **Shared Content** until you change it, including clearing it intentionally. **Remove Custom Override** appears only for edited captions and restores the shared text.
- **Channel settings** — each channel's section provides persistent provider configuration:

| Platform | Controls configured with the post                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| YouTube  | **Video Title** (required, 2–100 characters) and **Tags** (comma-separated; combined length limited to 500 characters, count capped by channel capabilities).                                                                                                                                                    |
| TikTok   | **Post Title** (optional), **Privacy Level** (required — options determined by creator account requirements), interaction toggles (**Duet**, **Stitch**, and **Comments** — enabled when supported by the creator account), and disclosure toggles (**Brand Content**, **Organic Brand**, and **AI Generated**). |
| Threads  | Informational panel — Threads posts use standard post text and attached media with no provider-specific controls.                                                                                                                                                                                                |

Facebook and Instagram channels publish using standard post text and attached media without provider-specific configuration panels in the composer.

### Validation

When you submit, the composer checks the post before anything is sent: a Brand Profile must be selected and the post needs text or media. Scheduling or publishing additionally requires at least one selected channel (and a valid date/time for **Schedule**) — **Save Draft** has neither requirement. Each channel's text must also fit that platform's limits (caption length, hashtag count, accepted media type, YouTube title, TikTok privacy level, and so on). Every problem is listed in a banner — fix them and submit again. Your text, media, and settings are kept even if submission fails.

With functional cookie consent, the composer can keep browser-local text, attachment, and override recovery for the active workspace/profile. Without that optional storage, composition and server-backed draft saving still work; do not rely on browser recovery after leaving. Successful scheduling or publication clears the local recovery copy. See [Cookies and storage](/docs/cookies).

## Draft, schedule, or publish now

- **Save Draft** — stores the post text without sending it anywhere. No channel selection is needed. Drafts appear in the **Drafts** status filter in Posts and can be edited, scheduled, or deleted later. Saving a draft for a post that was scheduled cancels its pending deliveries.
- **Schedule** — queues the post for the date and time in the picker.
- **Publish Now** — queues the post for immediate delivery to every selected channel.

"Queued" means Meltiply accepted the post for each channel; delivery to each platform then proceeds per channel and is reported individually — one channel can publish while another is still processing or has failed.

Choose **Schedule**, then open **Date** to pick a day from the inline calendar. Use **Today** or the month arrows to navigate; keyboard users can move with arrow keys, Home/End, or Page Up/Down and select with Enter. Choose the hour and exact minute using the 24-hour time controls. The browser's local time zone is shown below the picker. Changing the date preserves the time, changing time preserves the date, and browsing the calendar does not change the selected schedule. Calendar entry points retain their pre-filled date and 10:00 local time.

Every post gets its own individual date and time — Meltiply does not currently offer recurring weekly time-slot Queues, and the composer has no Queue mode that auto-assigns publication times from a named schedule.

## Post statuses

Each post shows a status per channel in the post details (open any post to see them). The group badge summarizes the most urgent channel state.

| Status                                  | Meaning                                                                                                   |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Scheduled                               | Waiting for its scheduled time.                                                                           |
| Queued / Processing / Awaiting provider | Delivery is in progress for that channel. Statuses update automatically while posts are in flight.        |
| Published                               | Delivered to the channel. A **View on platform** link opens the live post when the platform provides one. |
| Failed                                  | Delivery failed for that channel — the reason is shown.                                                   |
| Cancellation requested                  | Meltiply is stopping an in-flight delivery — check whether it still published on the platform.            |
| Cancelled                               | The delivery was stopped before it went out.                                                              |
| Needs reconciliation                    | Meltiply could not confirm the outcome with the platform.                                                 |

## Managing posts

Click a Table row, Grid card, or Calendar Post chip—or choose **Details** in a Calendar day's list—to open the side drawer. Its compact header identifies the Post and exposes supported actions. Flat properties, the full caption, ordered attachments, and real Channel outcomes share one scrollable body; this is not an editable task/status panel.

Use **Expand panel** for a wider centered inspector or **Restore side panel** to return to the edge. Phones use the full viewport. The body scrolls independently; close with the header's **Close** icon, **Escape**, or a click outside. Focus returns to the opening control.

Table has no trailing actions column. Open Details and choose **Edit post** for an editable Post; the header's **Post actions** menu offers **Delete draft**/**Delete post** and, when applicable, **Cancel all pending**, with confirmation. Grid cards and Calendar day entries retain direct Edit/Delete shortcuts. Provider publication outcomes cannot be manually marked complete.

Available actions depend on the channel's state:

- **Retry** — re-queues delivery for a **Failed** channel. Not every failure is retryable; when it isn't, the retry is refused with an error toast and the failure stays on the post.
- **Reschedule** — picks a new date and time for a delivery that hasn't published yet (also offered for unconfirmed deliveries).
- **Cancel** / **Cancel all pending** — stops pending deliveries. For a channel already in flight, this sends a cancellation request rather than a guaranteed stop — watch the status and check the platform. A confirmation dialog explains that only pending channels are cancelled; channels that already published are unaffected and the post stays live there. Cancelling never unpublishes a post that already went out.
- **Edit post** — opens a draft or a Post with eligible pending channels in the composer. Removing a channel removes its pending delivery. Published Channel outcomes remain observed results and cannot be manually rewritten.
- **Delete post / Delete draft** — removes the stored Post and stops its pending channels after confirmation. Anything already published remains on its platform; remove that copy on the platform itself.

> **Heads-up on unconfirmed deliveries:** if a channel shows **Awaiting provider** or **Needs reconciliation**, check the post directly on that platform before sending it again — the delivery may have actually gone through, and reposting could create a duplicate.
