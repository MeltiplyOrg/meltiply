Meltiply's community version does not impose quotas on monthly uploads,
per-channel publication distributions, connected accounts, or workspace storage.
You administer the infrastructure and are responsible for its capacity, usage,
and costs. There are no subscription tiers, checkout sessions, or billing portals
in the community application.

## Community allowances

| Resource                          | Community allowance |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Monthly uploads                   | Unlimited           |
| Monthly publication distributions | Unlimited           |
| Connected Channels per Workspace  | Unlimited           |
| Workspace storage                 | Unlimited           |

Unlimited allowances do not provide unlimited infrastructure. Monitor database,
object-storage, processing, queue, and provider usage and provision capacity for
your workload. Infrastructure failures and external service quotas still apply.

## Accounting and safeguards

Usage counters and storage inventories remain active. Storage accounting includes
originals, variants, temporary copies, and live upload or processing reservations.
Cleanup releases bytes only when the corresponding objects can be safely removed.
These records track resource consumption; they do not cap community usage.

Per-file media validation, provider restrictions, authentication safeguards, and
bounded processing remain in place. A Brand Profile still has one Channel per
provider; create more Brand Profiles to organize additional connections.

Quota enforcement remains available for a future hosted version with a bounded
resource policy. Those allowances are not applied to community deployments.
