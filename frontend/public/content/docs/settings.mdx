Open **Settings** in the sidebar or mobile navigation to visit `/settings`. The full-height page keeps the compact workspace rail, title/search bar, and current account controls available. Settings section navigation is a vertical rail on desktop and a horizontal row on phones.

## Appearance

Choose **Light** or **Dark**. The selected card is marked and the shared palette changes immediately without moving the page or resetting your work.

Meltiply uses warm off-white pages, a stone sidebar, compact controls, and indigo primary actions. Dark mode keeps the same layout with warm charcoal surfaces and a periwinkle accent. The palette is shared by the application, landing page, and these guides.

A current-page theme choice works without optional storage. Remembering it across visits requires **functional cookie consent**. See [Cookies and storage](/docs/cookies). Without an explicit choice, initial appearance follows your device's color scheme.

The publishing workspace uses immediate navigation and reduced-motion presentation rather than a separate Low motion switch. Theme and layout do not expose a custom accent or density control.

## Language

Select a supported interface language from **Language**. The current app supports English and Spanish; changing language applies immediately. Remembering it across visits requires functional cookie consent.

## Account

The Account section displays your name, email, and workspace as read-only details. It also provides **Sign out**, including on smaller screens. The sidebar shows your account avatar when one is supplied, or a user-icon fallback; it does not invent an avatar from your email.

The workspace name at the top of the rail is an identity label, not a workspace switcher. No plan tier is shown or implied.

## Security

Open the Security section, then **Account security**, to change your password, enroll an authenticator, or replace recovery codes. These actions require your current password. Enabling an authenticator requires verification before enrollment is complete; save recovery codes before finishing. Replacing codes invalidates the old set.

Security changes sign out previous sessions. The security dialog explains when a fresh sign-in is required. Keep passwords, setup keys, and recovery codes private.

## Brand Profiles and connected accounts

Brand Profiles are named scopes inside your workspace that own Channels and Posts. Select a profile in the sidebar or a collection's Brand Profile filter; **All Brand Profiles** returns to aggregate review. Profile selection is not workspace switching.

Workspace admins manage profiles from the **Channels** page:

- **Create Brand Profile** opens the profile form; saving a new profile opens the account connection flow.
- **Edit profile** in the Brand Profile filter opens the existing profile form.
- The form includes a name, optional description, and profile color.
- **Delete profile** requires confirmation and explains the consequences.

Use the Accounts directory to connect, re-authenticate, or disconnect platform accounts. Provider-specific configuration remains beside the affected account or Post, not in a generic settings panel.

## Scope of Settings

Settings is a preference and account route, not an administration console. It does not include billing, team invitations, notification configuration, API-key management, or self-service account identity editing. Reference-design examples do not imply these capabilities.
