Open **Settings** from the sidebar footer or the top bar. Settings opens over your current work, so you can inspect preferences without leaving Posts or Channels. On a phone, use the top-bar Settings icon.

## Appearance

Choose **Light** or **Dark** in the Appearance section. The selected card is marked, and the palette changes immediately without moving the page or resetting your work.

Meltiply uses warm off-white pages, a stone sidebar, compact controls, and indigo primary actions. Dark mode keeps the same layout with warm charcoal surfaces and a periwinkle accent. The palette is shared by the application, landing page, and these guides.

A current-page theme choice works without optional storage. Remembering it across visits requires **functional cookie consent**. See [Cookies and storage](/docs/cookies). Without an explicit choice, initial appearance follows your device's color scheme.

The publishing workspace uses immediate navigation and reduced-motion presentation rather than a separate Low motion switch. The interface renders in English; Settings does not expose a language selector or custom accent/density controls.

## Account

The Account section displays your name, email, and workspace as read-only details. It also provides **Sign out**, including on smaller screens. The sidebar shows your account avatar when one is supplied, or a user-icon fallback; it does not invent an avatar from your email.

The workspace name at the top of the rail is an identity label, not a workspace switcher. No plan tier is shown or implied.

## Security

Open the Security section, then **Account security**, to change your password, enroll an authenticator, or replace recovery codes. These actions require your current password. Enabling an authenticator requires verification before enrollment is complete; save recovery codes before finishing. Replacing codes invalidates the old set.

Security changes sign out previous sessions. The security dialog explains when a fresh sign-in is required. Keep passwords, setup keys, and recovery codes private.

## Brand Profiles

Brand Profiles are named scopes inside your workspace that own Channels and Posts. Select a profile in the sidebar or a collection's Brand Profile selector; **All Brand Profiles** returns to aggregate review. Profile selection is not workspace switching.

Workspace admins manage profiles through [Channels](/docs/channels) and [Publishing](/docs/publishing):

- **Create Brand Profile** opens the profile form; saving a new profile opens the account connection flow.
- **Edit profile** in the Brand Profile selector opens the existing profile.
- The form includes name, optional description, and a profile color.
- **Delete profile** requires confirmation and explains the consequences.

## Connected accounts

Use [Channels](/docs/channels) to connect, re-authenticate, or disconnect platform accounts. Provider-specific configuration remains beside the affected account or Post, not in a generic settings panel.

## Scope of Settings

Settings is a dialog, not a separate `/settings` administration route. It does not include billing, team invitations, notifications configuration, API-key management, or self-service account identity editing. Reference-design examples do not imply these capabilities exist.
