## 8. Scheduling, publishing, and delivery

- **Queued means accepted, not delivered.** Meltiply accepts the post for each selected channel and delivery proceeds per channel — one channel can publish while another is still processing or has failed. Per-channel statuses are tracked in the post details; see [Publishing posts](/docs/publishing).
- **A scheduled time is a target, not a guarantee.** Delivery depends on each platform — authorizations lapse, platform APIs change or go down, and platforms can reject or delay posts.
- **Failure states are visible.** Failed deliveries show a reason and can often be retried, though not every failure is retryable.
- **Outcomes can be uncertain.** If a channel shows **Awaiting provider** or **Needs reconciliation**, Meltiply could not confirm the result — check the platform before resending, or you may create a duplicate.
- **Cancellation is a request, not a recall.** Cancelling stops pending deliveries; for a delivery already in flight it sends a cancellation request that may not stop in time. Cancelling never unpublishes a post that already went out — only the platform can remove it.
