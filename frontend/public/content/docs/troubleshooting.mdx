Most issues have a safe, self-serve fix. Work through the symptom you're seeing below — none of these steps delete your posts or drafts.

## Publishing

### The action buttons are disabled in the composer

- Select one owning **Brand Profile** in the collection filter or composer. All Brand Profiles is for aggregate review.
- Add text or media. **Save Draft** does not require a destination; Schedule and Publish Now require at least one eligible selected channel.
- If the Post belongs to another profile, use the profile named by the composer before changing its publishing targets.

### A channel can't be selected in Destinations

Unavailable channels show a label on their pill:

- **Expired** — the platform login needs renewal. Go to [Channels](/docs/channels) and use **Re-authenticate** on that account.
- **Disabled** — the channel was disconnected or its access revoked. Reconnect it from [Channels](/docs/channels).
- **Incomplete** — the connection flow wasn't finished. Re-run the connection.

Hover the pill to see the reason.

### The composer lists errors when I submit

The banner lists every problem to fix before submitting. Common ones:

- **Empty post** — add text or at least one media item.
- **Media limit or type error** — the destination channel limits how many attachments can be published or restricts combining media types. Adjust the channel's attachment selection or deselect that channel.
- **Text too long** — the channel's platform caption limit is shown in the message; edit its caption directly in the per-channel section to shorten just that destination.
- **YouTube** — add a Video Title between 2 and 100 characters and keep tags within the limits.
- **TikTok** — pick a Privacy Level; if a toggle (Duet, Stitch, Comments) is blocked, that account doesn't allow it.
- **Media type** — the attached file isn't accepted by that channel (for example, a video where the platform expects an image); swap the media or deselect that channel.

If submission itself fails, everything you wrote is kept — fix the cause and submit again.

### Media constraints block scheduling or publishing

Each platform enforces media limits (such as single-video restrictions or maximum attachment counts). Switch to the channel's tab in the composer to review its attachment selection and adjust which files are included.

### My post is stuck on Queued, Processing, or Awaiting provider

These are normal in-progress states — posts update automatically while delivery is in flight, so give it a moment. If a channel sits there for an extended time, open the post details and **check whether the post actually went live on that platform first**. You can then use **Cancel** for that channel: cancellation is a request for an in-flight delivery, not a guaranteed stop, so verify on the platform before publishing the post again.

### A post failed to publish

1. Open the post and read the failure reason on the failed channel.
2. Use **Retry** on that channel — retrying only re-queues the failed channel, not the ones that published. If the failure isn't retryable, Meltiply refuses the retry and the failure stays; fix the cause (for example the channel's connection) and submit the post again instead.
3. If it fails again, check the channel itself: an expired or revoked connection blocks delivery. Re-authenticate on [Channels](/docs/channels), then retry.

### A post says "Needs reconciliation"

The outcome couldn't be confirmed with the platform — the post may or may not have been published. **Check the post on the platform itself before doing anything else**, otherwise you could publish a duplicate. If it didn't go live, cancel the unconfirmed delivery and submit the post again (for example by editing it and re-scheduling).

### I can't edit a Post or remove a published platform copy

Editing requires a draft or eligible pending/unconfirmed Channel delivery. Open Details and review the available header actions. Deleting the stored Post is a separate confirmed action: it stops pending delivery but leaves published copies on their platforms. Remove those copies on the platform itself. Cancelling an in-flight delivery is a request, not a guarantee that it did not publish.

### My draft disappeared

Drafts live under the **Drafts** status filter in [Posts](/docs/publishing). If you scheduled or published a post that used the draft, the draft version is replaced by the real post. If you can't find it at all, check the Brand Profile filter — drafts only show under the profile they were written for.

## Channels

### Connecting a channel fails

Connecting redirects you to the platform, then returns to Meltiply. Facebook opens a Page chooser: select a Page and **Connect Page** before expecting it in Channels. No eligible Pages means required permissions or Page access are missing. For connection limits, review [Resource limits](/docs/resource-limits) and free capacity where appropriate; there is no in-app plan upgrade flow. Retry while the selection is valid, or start Connect again after expiry.

If you cancelled the provider's permission prompt or authorization failed, run **Connect** or **Re-authenticate** again. A platform that can't be connected right now reports that its connection isn't available yet; try again later.

## Audience interactions and comments

The application does not currently expose an in-app inbox interface. The backend comment syncing capabilities and platform endpoints remain intact, but audience comments and replies should be managed directly on each connected social platform.

For information on comment processing, see [Inbox](/docs/inbox).

## Performance analytics

Posts **Table** includes stored engagement counters; use **Columns** to show or hide them. A `-` means the Post is not published, has no collected measurement, or that metric is untracked—not a measured zero. Saves and Clicks are currently untracked. Values follow backend synchronization rather than updating live. There is no dedicated analytics dashboard; use native platform reports for unsupported metrics.

For details on how analytics processing operates, see [Analytics](/docs/analytics).

## Media and uploads

If an upload fails, the attachment card shows a failed status in the composer and an error toast appears. Common causes are an interrupted network connection or exceeding a [resource limit](/docs/resource-limits). Select **Retry** on the attachment card using the local file, or re-attach the file. See [Media](/docs/media).

## App-level errors

Banners and errors you'll see anywhere in the app:

- **"You are offline"** — your connection dropped; changes are paused until it returns.
- **"Your session expired"** — sign in again; your posts and drafts are unaffected.
- **"Your session no longer has access to this workspace"** — your access changed; sign in again or ask the workspace owner about your membership.
- **"Meltiply is temporarily unavailable"** — maintenance or an outage; try again later.
- **"Meltiply could not refresh this view"** — a screen failed to load; use **Try again** on the error panel or reload the page.
- **"An error has occurred."** — a generic failure; retry the action.
- **"Too many requests. Wait a moment and try again."** — rate limiting; pause briefly and retry.
- **"This changed while you were working. Refresh and try again."** — something was edited elsewhere (another tab or teammate); reload the screen and redo your change.
- **"Your storage or upload quota has been reached."** — check [Resource limits](/docs/resource-limits) for workspace quotas.
- **"That item is no longer available."** — the post, draft, or asset was deleted since you opened it; go back to the list.
- **"Choose an active workspace to continue."** — the app does not offer an in-app workspace switcher; reload the page, then sign out and back in so your session can resolve a workspace again.

## Still stuck?

If a problem persists: reload the page, then sign out and back in. For failed posts use the built-in **Retry** in the post details. If nothing works, contact support with a description of what you tried.
