Meltiply organizes everything you do into two levels: a **workspace** at the top, and **Brand Profiles** inside it. Understanding this structure makes every other screen easier to use.

## Workspaces

Your workspace is the organization your account belongs to — typically your company, agency, or personal business. Its name appears at the top of the sidebar as a read-only identity label.

The app does not offer workspace switching or a plan-tier selector. Your active sign-in determines the organization boundary. Inspect account details and sign out from **Settings → Account**, or use the sidebar footer.

Select a Brand Profile in the Filter disclosure on Posts or Channels, or find it through top-bar search. Profiles are not sidebar sections. Selecting one scopes Posts and Channels; **All Brand Profiles** returns to aggregate review.

## Brand Profiles

A **Brand Profile** is a named, color-labeled scope inside your workspace. Every channel connection, media asset, and post belongs to exactly one Brand Profile.

Use Brand Profiles to separate things you manage independently — for example, one profile per client, brand, product line, or region. Each profile scopes Posts and Channels, helping you avoid cross-posting to the wrong audience.

Each profile has:

- **Profile name** (required, up to 100 characters)
- **Description** (optional, up to 280 characters — shown on Brand Profile cards and in relevant empty states)
- **Color** — a swatch that identifies the profile's channels and posts across the app

### Who can manage profiles

- **Workspace admins** can create, edit, and delete Brand Profiles.
- **Members** can view and select profiles through Channels' Brand Profiles tab, the Brand Profile filter on Channels/Posts, or jump search. They cannot create, edit, or delete profiles; if you need a profile, ask an admin.

### Create a Brand Profile

Workspace admins create Brand Profiles directly on Channels:

1. Open the **Brand Profiles** tab.
2. Select **Create Brand Profile** in the Channels header.
3. Enter a **Profile name** (required), an optional **Description**, and choose a color swatch.
4. Select **Create profile** to save.

After creation, Meltiply selects the new profile and opens the account connection dialog so you can link a channel.

### Edit a profile

Admins can edit a Brand Profile from Channels or its filter:

1. Open the **Brand Profiles** tab on Channels and select its edit action, or open the **Brand Profile** filter on Channels/Posts.
2. Update the name, description, or color in the editor, then save.

Editing updates profile metadata immediately and does not alter connected channels or existing posts.

### Delete a profile

Deleting a Brand Profile permanently removes the profile and its associated channels, media assets, and posts:

1. Open the profile editor from the Brand Profiles card on Channels, or its edit action in the Brand Profile filter on Posts.
2. Select **Delete** and confirm the irreversible action.

Deleting stops scheduled and queued posts on that profile's channels.

### Soft-deleted profiles

The backend retains soft-deleted Brand Profiles for 7 days before permanent purge. Backend restore operations can restore profile records, but disconnected channels require re-authorization on the Channels page. Explicit deletion confirms permanent removal.

## The active Brand Profile selection

Choose a Brand Profile in the **Filter** disclosure on Channels or Posts to control which profile's work you see:

- **All Brand Profiles** — the default aggregate view showing everything
- A specific profile — Posts and Channels collections show that profile's items, and profile-owned actions stay scoped to their owner.

The selection matters because some actions only work with a profile chosen:

- **Publishing or scheduling** targets one explicitly selected profile. A channel's publication outcome comes from the provider and is not an editable profile setting.

Channel disconnect and Post deletion, individually or in selected batches, use
each item's owning Brand Profile and also work in **All Brand Profiles**. Deleting
a Post stops its pending channels but leaves already-published platform copies intact.

The selection is shared across screens — picking a profile on Channels keeps it active on Posts. Choose **All Brand Profiles** to return to the aggregate view.
If a channel or post still refers to a profile that no longer resolves in your workspace — for example one awaiting purge — it appears under **Unknown profile** so the item never disappears silently.

## Roles at a glance

| Action                          | Admin | Member |
| ------------------------------- | ----- | ------ |
| Select a Brand Profile filter   | Yes   | Yes    |
| Create / edit / delete profiles | Yes   | No     |
| Connect and reconnect channels  | Yes   | Yes    |
| Attach and upload media         | Yes   | Yes    |
